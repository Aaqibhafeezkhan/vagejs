# Code of conduct

vagejs is a small open-source project, and we want everyone who uses it or helps with it to feel welcome: beginners, experts, and people using AI assistants or writing every line by hand.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and remember that many people here are new to building websites.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person. Say what's wrong and, if you can, how to fix it.
- Respect different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience.
- Credit other people's work, and don't share anyone's private information.

## What isn't accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's identity, background or body.
- Sexual content or unwelcome attention.
- Spam, including promotional links unrelated to the discussion.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.

## Where this applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions), on vagejs.com, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Email **vage@vagejs.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept private. For security problems in the code, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who break these rules, starting with a warning when the problem is minor.
